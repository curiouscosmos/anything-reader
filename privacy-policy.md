# Privacy Policy for Anything Reader

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Anything Reader is designed as a local-first reading and listening app. Your imported books, documents, notes, RSS content, generated audio, and playback state are stored on your device unless you choose to share them through a feature that requires external services.

## Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to understand app usage, improve reliability, and diagnose crashes.

### Information collected automatically

When you use Anything Reader, we may collect:

- app version and build number
- operating system version
- device model and hardware information
- crash reports and diagnostic information
- performance and usage events, such as screen views, feature interactions, and app lifecycle events
- approximate technical identifiers needed by analytics or crash reporting services

### Information we do not intentionally collect

Anything Reader does not intentionally collect:

- the text of your imported books, documents, or pasted notes for analytics purposes
- the contents of your local library for advertising or profiling
- payment information
- contacts
- photos
- precise location

## How We Use Information

We use collected data to:

- understand how the app is used
- improve app stability and performance
- diagnose and fix bugs
- monitor crashes and unexpected failures
- decide which features need improvement

We do not use collected data to sell advertising or build advertising profiles.

## Third-Party Services

Anything Reader uses the following third-party services:

### PostHog

PostHog is used for analytics so we can understand feature usage and improve the app. PostHog may receive event data and associated technical metadata from the app.

### Sentry

Sentry is used for crash reporting and error monitoring. Sentry may receive crash logs, stack traces, diagnostic information, and related technical metadata when an error occurs.

These providers process data on our behalf to help us operate and improve Anything Reader.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited technical data with our analytics and crash-reporting providers, as described above, only for the purpose of operating, maintaining, and improving the app.

We may also disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the app.

## Data Retention

We retain analytics and crash data only for as long as needed to support the purposes described in this policy, subject to the retention practices of our service providers.

Local files you import into Anything Reader remain on your device unless you explicitly export, share, or otherwise move them elsewhere.

## Your Choices

You can reduce or stop data collection by:

- stopping use of the app
- disabling analytics or crash reporting where supported by your system or service settings
- deleting the app and its local data from your device

If you want to request deletion of data associated with analytics or crash reporting, contact us using the details below.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children’s Privacy

Anything Reader is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it if appropriate.

## International Transfers

If you access Anything Reader from outside the country where our service providers operate, your information may be processed in other jurisdictions that may have different data protection laws.

## Changes To This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
